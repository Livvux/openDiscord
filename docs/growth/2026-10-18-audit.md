# Commercial search baseline — 18 October 2026

## Business decision

Prioritize community owners evaluating a controlled pilot or a self-hosted deployment. openDiscord is AGPL-3.0, closed alpha, with voluntary sponsorship; no paid subscription or revenue funnel was found. Optimize approved and activated community owners, not raw account requests. Self-hosting interest is a separate outcome and must not be counted as hosted signup revenue.

## Prioritization

The CSV contains 40 query hypotheses; five have the direct Google observations above. Do not confuse discovery with search-volume validation. Scores are editorial estimates, not keyword-difficulty data or predicted conversion rates. Intent/relevance/conversion fit: 1 low–5 high. Competition and effort: 1 low–5 high. Score = 3×intent + 3×relevance + 2×conversion_fit − competition − effort. Sort descending, then break ties by direct evidence and reusable page coverage. Group variants into useful pages rather than publishing 40 thin pages.

1. Ship the comparison and hosting pages; expose approval requirements before the signup CTA. Use existing real demo assets and cite competitor advantages. Sources/prices dated in copy. These are branch changes until merged and deployed.
2. Establish measurement access and a meaningful activation definition before publishing more pages. Avoid using free signup requests as customers/revenue.
3. Only after evidence: expand voice/network deployment decisions and specific competitor comparisons. Integration queries need a real working example; security/enterprise claims need acceptance evidence the alpha does not yet have.

## Measurement and monthly operating procedure

Baseline statuses live in `measurement-baseline.csv`; blank numeric fields mean unavailable. Do not fill them with estimates.

1. Grant the existing reporting account access to the GSC domain property, then verify `gsc sites`, inspect canonical/indexability for the two deployed pages and submit the actual sitemap. Keep credentials private. GSC does not itself attribute AI referrals or signup activation.
2. Define a qualified hosted conversion as: approved account + creates a community + a second member participates within seven days. Keep signup requests, approval, activation and sponsorship receipts separate. This is a proposed product definition, not a metric already collected.
3. Before implementing analytics, agree on first-party aggregate attribution consistent with the no-trackers privacy promise. Record only allowlisted landing path, source category (Google/Bing/ChatGPT/Perplexity/direct/other) and aggregate conversion counts; no message content, tokens, full query strings, email addresses or fingerprinting. Referrers can be missing, so “direct” is not proof of no AI influence. Cross-device attribution remains uncertain.
4. Report weekly: organic landing clicks, alpha requests, approvals, activated communities, self-host guide visits, verified deployments and actual sponsorship revenue. Guide clicks are a proxy, not installations; sponsorship revenue is not paid-hosting MRR. No current counts available through the inspected interfaces.
5. Every 30 days, rerun the five exact queries above and next five highest-scoring queries using the same country/language/device. Save UTC time, engine, exact prompt, final answer state, visible sources, brand mentions, owned-page citation, canonical destination and evidence path. Mark challenges/authentication failures as blocked. Test ChatGPT Search, Perplexity and Gemini only through accessible actual answers.
6. At 30 days after deployment compare GSC page/query cohorts with the prelaunch period, annotate launch/indexing dates and small samples. At 60–90 days prioritize pages producing activated owners or attributable contributions. If impressions rise without qualified activation, revise qualification/CTA before expanding content. No guaranteed lift or statistical certainty assumed.

It does not authorize automatic production deployment or outreach messages. This interval is not a calendar-month schedule. Expansion depends on observed outcomes, not a preset volume of articles.
